The Practice

Dr. Warren Patch

Native San Diegan. Lifelong surfer. Licensed chiropractor since 1982. His family practice on Sunset Cliffs Boulevard has quietly cared for Ocean Beach and Point Loma for four decades.

Equally at home with infants, athletes, and elders, Dr. Patch works with an intuitive, highly refined touch — informed by science, second-degree Reiki, and decades of hands-on experience. The adjustment is never rushed. The conversation is always real.

When the spine moves freely, everything else follows.
